About

Photos as the start of a story.

StoryInColor is an editorial studio that turns a single photo (your palm, your face, your handwriting, your style, your skin) into a magazine-quality reading. One reading, designed to be saved.

01 / Editorial

Written like a magazine, not an algorithm.

Each reading is composed like an editorial feature, typeset with intention, paced for a quiet read, and finished to print quality. The voice is warm and restrained: never breathless, never cold.

02 / Honest

Reflective, not predictive.

We frame readings as cultural and lifestyle reflections: palmistry as tradition, beauty as observation, style as expression. Where wellness brushes the edges of medicine, we say so plainly.

03 / Saveable

Made to keep.

Every reading is exported in a high-resolution, print-ready format. Some end up framed. Some end up on the fridge. Either is the right answer.

What we make

Many readings, one voice.

From mystical traditions like palmistry and Mian Xiang face reading, to data-driven reports like the beauty report and skincare glow, to lifestyle reflections like style audit and graphology, every reading is finished in the same editorial register.

Behind the work

Built with care.

Each reading is composed by a long-form prompt tuned for editorial layout. Thin hairlines, generous whitespace, refined typography. The prompts were written by hand and are revised the same way magazine art direction is revised: print, look, edit, repeat.

We don't sell your photos. Uploads stay on your account so you can re-download a reading you loved, and you can delete everything from your settings at any time.

Most readings finish in up to 2 minutes. If something goes wrong on our end, the reading is refunded to your balance automatically, no support ticket required.
