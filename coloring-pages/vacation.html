What kind of photo works best

Any well-lit vacation photo with clear focal points. Beach shots, city skylines, landmark photos, group photos in front of recognisable scenery, and food photos from special restaurants all work. Sharper photos produce cleaner line-art.

What you can make

  • Beach and ocean photos
  • City skylines and landmarks
  • Mountain landscapes
  • Hotel and Airbnb scenery
  • Food and restaurant photos
  • Group vacation photos

Who it’s for

Travel keepsake collectors, anyone planning a scrapbook, parents making vacation memory books with kids, retirees archiving travel memories.
