2010s hairstyles

Beachy waves, balayage, and the sleek low pony

The 2010s were defined by texture and natural-looking color. Jen Atkin's beachy lob anchored most of the decade. Balayage replaced foil highlights as the default salon color technique. The sleek low pony took over the red carpet in the late 2010s, and the textured top knot took over Saturday mornings. The look was undone, expensive, and engineered to read effortless.

How to wear it today

The 2010s aesthetic is still the dominant look on red carpets today. Balayage is still the default color request. Beachy lobs are still the most-cut mid-length shape.
