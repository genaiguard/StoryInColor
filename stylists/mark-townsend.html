Mark Townsend

The stylist behind the modern undone look

Mark Townsend works with a long list of red-carpet clients including the Olsen twins, Dakota Johnson, and Elle Fanning. His signature is undone hair that reads polished without looking set: tousled waves, loose buns, and the kind of texture-led styling that has dominated red-carpet hair since the early 2010s. He works closely with Dove as a brand ambassador and writes about styling technique frequently.

Why their work matters

Townsend's contribution has been to make undone hair credible at the highest visibility level. His clients walk red carpets with what looks like the way they wore their hair to lunch, and that gap between formality and effort has shaped how a generation of stylists thinks about premiere styling. The tousled middle part on long hair is now a default rather than a statement, and Townsend's work is most of why.
