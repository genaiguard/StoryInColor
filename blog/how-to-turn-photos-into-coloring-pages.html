How to Turn ANY Photo Into a Custom Coloring Page (Even If You're Technically Challenged)

By David ParkJanuary 25, 202412 minute read

My Embarrassing Photo-to-Coloring Page Disaster


I remember my first attempt like it was yesterday. I spent 3 hours in Photoshop trying to manually trace a photo of my daughter. My hands were cramped, my eyes were crossed, and the result looked like a toddler's finger painting.


I almost gave up on the whole idea.


But then I discovered StoryInColor. And let me tell you - if I can do this, ANYONE can do this.


The "I Hate Technology" Method That Actually Works


Forget everything you think you know about photo editing. This isn't about being artistic or technical. This is about preserving memories in the simplest way possible.


Here's the truth: You don't need design skills, expensive software, or hours of your time. You just need a decent photo and 5 minutes.


Step 1: Pick a Photo That Makes You Smile (This Is the Secret!)


Don't overthink this. Grab any photo that sparks joy:


Perfect examples:

  • Your kid's first birthday cake face
  • That sunset from your dream vacation
  • Your dog looking guilty after eating your shoes
  • Grandma's famous apple pie recipe (the photo, not the actual pie)

The key: If it makes you happy looking at it, it'll make you happy coloring it.


Pro tip: Start with a photo that's well-lit. Dark or blurry photos are like trying to color with invisible ink.


Step 2: Sign Up (It's Literally 30 Seconds)


  • Go to StoryInColor.com
  • Click "Start Creating" (the big orange button you can't miss)
  • Type your email
  • Click the confirmation link in your inbox

Boom. You're in. And you get 2 FREE credits just for signing up. No credit card required.


Step 3: Upload Your Photo (Easier Than Emailing)


Method 1 - Drag and Drop:

  • Open your photo on your computer
  • Drag it onto the upload area
  • Watch the magic happen

Method 2 - Click to Browse:

  • Click "Choose Files"
  • Find your photo
  • Click "Open"

That's it. Seriously. If you can attach a photo to an email, you can do this.


Step 4: Choose Your Style (The Fun Part!)


Here's where you get creative:


Classic Style (My Go-To)

  • Clean, crisp lines
  • Perfect for detailed coloring
  • Great for photos with lots of elements

Studio Ghibli Style (Magical!)

  • Soft, dreamy lines
  • Inspired by animated films
  • Makes everything look like a storybook

My advice: Try both styles on the same photo. You'll be amazed at how different they look.


Step 5: Wait 30 Seconds (Go Make Coffee)


Our AI does all the heavy lifting. You'll see a progress bar, and in about 30 seconds, your personalized coloring page is ready.


What happens behind the scenes:

  • AI analyzes your photo
  • Converts colors to shades of gray
  • Creates clean, colorable lines
  • Optimizes for printing

Step 6: Download and Print (The Grand Finale)


Your coloring page downloads as a beautiful PDF. Print it on:

  • Cardstock (80-100 lb works best)
  • Regular printer paper (works fine too)
  • Whatever you have handy

Coloring tools I recommend:

  • Crayons (nostalgic and forgiving)
  • Colored pencils (for detail work)
  • Markers (for bold statements)

Common Questions (From Real People Like You)


"My photo is blurry. Will this still work?"

Our AI can work miracles, but super blurry photos will still look blurry. Try to use photos that are at least 1000x1000 pixels.


"Can I edit the coloring page after creation?"

You can't directly edit the lines, but you can create multiple versions with different styles. Think of it as having different "moods" of the same photo.


"What if I hate the result?"

No problem! Generate a new version with a different style, or try a different photo. We want you to love your coloring pages.


"How many photos can I upload at once?"

Upload as many as you want! Each coloring page costs 1 credit, but you can preview them all before deciding which ones to create.

Coloring became personal again.
